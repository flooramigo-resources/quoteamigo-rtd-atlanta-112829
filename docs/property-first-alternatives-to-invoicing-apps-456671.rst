Property-first alternatives to invoicing apps
=============================================

A conventional invoicing app usually begins with a customer and a blank estimate. QuoteAmigo begins with the property address, provides available property details and preliminary dimensions, and helps the contractor prepare the quote in WhatsApp. These comparisons separate that workflow from broader billing and accounting tools.
