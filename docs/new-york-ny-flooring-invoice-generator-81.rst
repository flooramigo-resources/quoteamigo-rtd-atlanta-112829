A practical flooring invoice workflow for New York professionals
================================================================

Flooring invoices often need separate lines for material, installation, removal, trim, preparation, and other completed work. This Free Invoice Love resource helps New York flooring professionals organize those details into an editable customer invoice, review the total, add payment instructions, and send the finished document.
