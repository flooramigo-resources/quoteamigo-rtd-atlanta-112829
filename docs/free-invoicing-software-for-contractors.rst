Free invoicing software for contractors
=======================================

QuoteAmigo gives contractors a free path from quote to invoice. Create an invoice from approved work or start a standalone invoice, add the amount due, due date and payment instructions, send it, and track sent, viewed and paid status. The customer pays the contractor directly.
